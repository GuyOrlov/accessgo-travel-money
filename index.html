THE PROPOSED EXPERIENCE

From your next trip to your eWallet.

We are proposing a partnership with Travelex. This is how we want the service to work, subject to agreement and technical checks.

  1. 01

    Choose and check

    Choose your currency. See the exchange rate, any fees and the amount you would receive before continuing.

  2. 02

    Pay with the provider

    Continue to Travelex to complete payment and identity checks. HasslePass would not handle your money.

  3. 03

    Receive your eVoucher

    The proposal is for Travelex to send a HasslePass eVoucher by email, ready to add to a supported digital wallet.

  4. 04

    Enjoy your trip

    The aim is to pay abroad and access cash at supported ATMs. Countries, wallets, fees and availability still need to be agreed.

FROM THE COMMUNITY

Built around real communication needs.

HasslePass is being developed by Spencer Collins and Guy Orlov. Spencer brings disability inclusion experience; Guy runs cSeeker, a British Sign Language interpreting agency.

Clarity at every step

  • Plain language and clear costs
  • Larger text and high-contrast controls
  • Keyboard access and screen-reader labels
  • Planned sign language, text and VRS support

A LITTLE HELP

Your questions, made simpler.

Can I order travel money now?

Not yet. This is a concept preview. It does not take payments, issue vouchers or connect to a live exchange service.

Is Travelex a confirmed partner?

No. The proposal sets out a possible partnership. Commercial terms and technical features still need to be agreed.

Is this a bank account or prepaid card?

The proposal is for a digital voucher for a specific currency exchange, rather than a bank account or a card you top up. The final product and its terms are not yet agreed.
