Terms and conditions

These terms apply when you use this demonstration website.

About this demo

HasslePass is a concept preview. It does not accept orders, reserve currency, process payments or issue valid eVouchers. A Travelex partnership is proposed, not confirmed. Wallet, ATM and spending features require agreement and technical validation.

Example information

Exchange rates, zero provider fees and voucher amounts are illustrative examples, not live Travelex offers. Supported countries, wallets, ATM access, fees, limits and validity have not been agreed.

Using the website

You may browse the site and try its example tools for lawful purposes. Do not try to damage the site, disrupt access or use it to mislead another person.

Privacy notice

This notice explains what happens to information when you use the current demo.

Information sent to HasslePass

There is no account, order, payment or contact form. The site owner does not receive personal information through this demo.

Your example quote

The current calculator and eVoucher preview run in your browser. They do not submit your quote or create an order. Older links may include an amount, currency and language in their address; those values can appear in browser history and hosting logs. Do not add personal or payment information.

Demo travel helper

Messages typed into the travel helper are matched to preset replies in your browser. HasslePass does not send or save those messages.

Choices saved on your device

Your language, larger-text and contrast choices can be saved in your browser. They stay on your device until you change them or clear this site’s stored data.

Website hosting

GitHub Pages hosts this demo. GitHub may process normal technical information such as IP address, device details, request time and pages viewed under its own privacy statement.

Your rights and a future live service

Before HasslePass collects personal information or becomes a live service, this notice will be updated with the data controller’s identity, a working privacy contact, purposes, lawful bases, retention periods and your rights.

  • Read GitHub’s privacy statement
  • Learn about your information rights from the ICO

Cookies and device storage

This section covers cookies and similar storage used by the current demo.

No advertising cookies

HasslePass code does not set cookies. It does not use analytics, advertising trackers or social-media pixels.

Local storage

After you choose a language, larger text or high contrast, the site uses local storage to remember that choice on your device. This is used only to provide the setting you asked for.

No visitor tracking

HasslePass does not create visitor counts, advertising profiles or cross-site tracking. If non-essential tracking is added later, clear information and suitable controls will be added first.

How to clear your choices

Use your browser settings to remove this site’s stored data. The website will then return to its default language, text size and contrast.

  • Read the ICO guidance on cookies and similar technologies

Accessibility statement

HasslePass aims to make this demo usable by as many people as possible.

Accessibility features

The site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, larger text, high contrast, plain language, right-to-left Hebrew and layouts that adapt to small screens.

Current limits

BSL videos and live human support are planned but are not available. This prototype has not had a formal independent accessibility audit.

Accessibility contact

A working accessibility contact and response process will be published before a live service launches.
